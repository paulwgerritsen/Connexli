Common questions

Does Connexli cost homeowners anything?

No. Connexli is free for homeowners. Professionals compete for your business, and you keep the savings that competition creates.

Is Connexli a real estate brokerage?

No. Connexli is the comparison and introduction layer. When you choose a professional, your listing agreement is signed directly with their brokerage, and they represent you from there.

How are professionals verified?

Every professional provides their real estate license number and brokerage when they sign up, and we confirm the license is active in their state before they can submit a single proposal.

Who sees my information?

Until you choose, professionals see only general details: area, home type, size, and price range. Your name, exact address, and contact info are released only to the professional you select.

When and where does Connexli launch?

The pilot opens in Davis, Salt Lake, and Utah counties, with more areas to follow. Waitlist members hear first, and founding professional spots are limited.
